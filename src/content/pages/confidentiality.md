Much of what clients send me is confidential: material that has not been published yet, and some that never will be. This page sets out how I handle it, including where AI is involved.

## The basics

- I sign NDAs and confidentiality agreements on request.
- Your documents are used for your job and nothing else.
- If your agreement sets out how documents must be handled, tell me before the job starts and I will work within it.

## How I use AI

I use AI where it helps, not for everything. When I do, it is in memoQ or Trados Studio through [Supervertaler](https://supervertaler.com), the translation software I develop myself. The AI produces a first draft. I review, correct and finalise every sentence, and what you receive is my work and my responsibility.

The only AI I use for client work is **Claude, from Anthropic**, and only under Anthropic's [Commercial Terms](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/commercial-terms), the terms for business customers:

- **Treated as confidential information.** The Commercial Terms class everything I send to Claude, and everything it sends back, as my confidential information. Anthropic may use it only to provide the service, and must protect it with at least reasonable care.
- **Not used for training.** Under the same terms, Anthropic may not train its models on it.
- **Deleted within 30 days.** Text sent through Anthropic's API is [automatically deleted within 30 days](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data), unless the law or a safety review requires it to be kept longer.
- **Sent directly.** Supervertaler sends the text straight from my computer to Anthropic. It does not pass through any other server, mine included.
- **A business plan, not a personal one.** I use Anthropic's API and a Claude Team plan, both covered by the Commercial Terms. Claude's personal plans (Free, Pro and Max) fall under consumer terms, where whether your conversations train future models is a setting. I moved to a Team plan so that client work is never under those terms.

## If your agreement says anything about AI

Some agreements restrict or rule out processing by outside services. Tell me before the job starts, and we will agree how to handle it.
